About this calendar

Two-week timetable cycle starting Monday 28 September 2026 (Week 1). One AI Literacy lesson per section per week, delivered through Arabic or Islamic Studies. Holidays, breaks and PD days come from the AISA 2026–27 calendar: when a section's AI period falls on a day with no school, its next lesson is pushed to its next period, and break weeks do not count towards the Week 1 / Week 2 cycle. Islamic holiday dates are still to be confirmed. Teacher names are kept off the public page.
