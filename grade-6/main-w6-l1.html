One connected chain

The system type shapes what you can expect. The prompt shapes the output. The output needs a triage – correct, incorrect or unclear. And the whole process needs a risk check.

Each strand feeds the next. Miss one link and the chain gets weaker.

Core · Part 2 of 3

Explain the links

How does one strand shape the next?

Explain the links

It is not enough to complete each step. You should be able to explain how one step shaped the next.

For example: because the prompt was specific, the output stayed on task, which made the triage easier and the risk lower.

Core · Part 3 of 3

A responsible choice

Which choices keep your use responsible?

Name a responsible choice

The final link is responsibility. You run a quick risk check and make a responsible-use choice you can name.

A responsible choice is clear and safe – like keeping personal data out of the prompt, or checking an important output against a trusted source before you share it.

Core mission · Worksheet

Run one task through all four strands

Be a system detective. Take one guided challenge through the whole chain, then explain how the system and prompt shaped the output and name one responsible-use choice you made.

Let's try it

What you need: a printed task card, prompt option cards, a curated output card, a four-strand chain organiser and a pencil.

  1. Name the system type behind the task on your organiser.
  2. Write or choose a clear, specific prompt.
  3. Triage the curated output as correct, incorrect or unclear.
  4. Run a quick risk check and note one responsible-use choice.
  5. Explain to your partner how the system and prompt shaped the output.
  6. Write which strand felt strongest and which needs practice.

Lab · Part 1 of 3

Build, then check

What order do the stations run in?

Build, then check

Building means choosing a suitable system and designing a clear prompt to reach a useful output.

Checking means triaging that output for accuracy and running a risk assessment before you decide to use it. Building without checking just makes confident mistakes.

Lab · Part 2 of 3

Two verdicts

Is each output correct, unclear or incorrect?

Judge for accuracy

Before you decide to use an output, you triage it for accuracy: correct, unclear or incorrect.

You judge it against what you know or a trusted source. A confident answer can still be wrong, and an unclear one needs more checking before you trust it.

Lab · Part 3 of 3

Decide and diagnose

What goes in your record, and where is your weak link?

Decide and diagnose

Your final call is use, do not use, or use-with-changes – and you must back it up with your two verdicts.

Then diagnose your weakest link: the strand where your evidence was thinnest. That is your focus before the signature solution.

Lab mission · Worksheet

Four stations, one build-and-check

Run one real task through four stations from start to end. Build it, check it for accuracy and risk, decide whether to use it, and diagnose the strand you most need to sharpen.

Let's try it

What you need: a four-station map, a printed task card, prompt option cards, a curated output card and a risk sheet.

  1. Station 1: name the system type that suits the task, and say why.
  2. Station 2: design a clear, specific prompt.
  3. Station 3: triage the curated output for accuracy, with a reason.
  4. Station 4: run a risk assessment and choose a mitigation.
  5. Give the output an accuracy verdict and a risk verdict, then decide: use, do not use, or use-with-changes.
  6. Diagnose your group's weakest strand before the signature solution.

Tool for this week: unplugged four-station pack

No device is needed. No device? Run all four stations unplugged with the printed task, prompt options, curated output card and risk sheet – the full rehearsal still works. Keep real names, phone numbers and addresses out of every prompt and every output you share.

Lesson summary

You wore all four hats at once, then ran the full build-and-check – your dress rehearsal for the signature solution

Key ideas to remember

  • Real AI fluency is one connected chain, not four separate skills.
  • The system type shapes the prompt, the prompt shapes the output, the output needs a triage, and the whole task needs a risk check.
  • You should be able to explain how one strand shaped the next.
  • Every task ends with a responsible-use choice you can name.
  • Building without checking makes confident mistakes; checking without building makes nothing.
  • Every output earns an accuracy verdict and a risk verdict before a use decision: use, do not use, or use-with-changes.
  • Your weakest link is the strand where your evidence was thinnest.

Glossary

Strand
one of the four skills: system type, prompt, triage and risk check.
Triage
judging an output as correct, incorrect or unclear.
Risk check
a quick look for what could go wrong before you use an output.
Responsible-use choice
a clear, safe decision you make, like keeping personal data out of a prompt.
Build
choosing a suitable system and designing a clear prompt to reach a useful output.
Check
triaging an output for accuracy and running a risk assessment before deciding.
Mitigation
a design choice that lowers a risk you have found.
Weakest link
the strand where your evidence was thinnest and most needs work.
